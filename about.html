ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Syna. I'm a computer science student at Arizona State with a data science minor, and I build machine learning systems and iOS apps. At Micron that meant a clustering pipeline that groups chip failures by root cause. On my own it is things like an iPhone app that learns a new object from a single photo.

As a kid I spent my summers at robotics and coding camps, because it was fun. I learned to code from Codemy.com tutorials, and hence the bald man (John Elder) on the cover of the spotify cover, one of the most influential figures in my life. The camps turned into projects, then internships and research, and I still get the same feeling when something I built finally works.

WHY MACHINE LEARNING

I love machine learning because it can make sense of messy things. Data often looks like noise at first, but a good model finds the pattern hidden inside it, and that pattern can help predict what comes next. What I keep chasing is the moment when something that made no sense finally starts to.

THE OCEAN

I love the ocean. Someday I want to swim with whales and see belugas and orcas in the wild. The one problem is that I hate the feeling of sand on my feet, so I am still working out how to love the ocean without the beach.

METAL EARTH

I build Metal Earth models. They prick your fingers, and you twist and pinch tiny wires until a flat sheet turns into something real. It feels like playing as much as building, and I like how it leaves me feeling, and having the finished model on my desk afterwards.

BOOKS

I have always read fiction. Lately I have been trying to get into classics and historical books, to grow my brain a bit.
